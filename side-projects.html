Evenings and weekends

FADE IN:

EXT. Tienen – 2009

OpgewekTienen. Co-founder, 2009. KEVIN and a friend start a small blog with good news about Tienen. Thirteen years later it is a citizens’ movement of more than 200 volunteers, with an official Flemish culture award. opgewektienen.org

EXT. Tienen – 2012

Gine IJle. Columns, 2012. A fortnightly column for Tienen Actueel, with a generous helping of Tienen dialect. The title means “that’s quite something”. Read about the columns.

INT. A fan archive – 2026

Mass Effect Universe. Fan site, 2026. An unofficial, non-commercial home for fans of the Mass Effect games: the story so far, where to start as a new player, and a place to gather. KEVIN writes and runs it under the name Legion. masseffectuniverse.com

INT. The lab – weekend

Lab. AI experiments. Small games Kevin builds with AI, and what each one taught him about AI in marketing. Visit the Lab.
