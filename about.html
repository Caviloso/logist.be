About Kevin Logist

I help organisations find their story and get it heard.

Based on a true story

FADE IN:

EXT. Tienen – 2009

The sugar town in the Hageland.

Kevin (V.O.)

I grew up in Tienen, and I live in Limburg now. In 2009 a friend and I started OpgewekTienen, a small blog with good news about Tienen. Thirteen years later it was a citizens’ movement of more than 200 volunteers, with an official Flemish culture award. That’s still the best proof I know of what a good story can do.

In 2012 I wrote a fortnightly column for Tienen Actueel, with a generous helping of Tienen dialect. It was called Gine IJle, and it’s where the storytelling started.

Montage – twenty years

  • A) 2004. Groep T. Fresh from a teaching degree, KEVIN lectures for almost two years.
  • B) 2006. Voka – Chamber of Commerce & Industry Leuven. He builds a training programme for SMEs, runs the PLATO peer-learning programme and spends six and a half years as editor-in-chief of Ondernemers, the magazine for entrepreneurs. He stays for ten and a half years.
  • C) 2017. The drones arrive. At EUKA he builds the community for Belgium’s young drone industry.
  • D) 2019. At DroneMatrix he runs marketing and communications through the COVID period and rebrands the company.
  • E) November 2021. Akkodis. He launches the Akkodis brand in the Benelux after the AKKA–Modis merger, and takes a seat on the global marketing and communications leadership teams.
  • F) April 2024. He also becomes Country Head of Communications for The Adecco Group in Belgium: corporate, internal and crisis communications.

END MONTAGE.

INT. A meeting room – day

A whiteboard, a team and a blank page. KEVIN uncaps a marker.

Kevin

(counting on his fingers)

One: brand building from scratch, inside the organisation as much as outside it.

Two: complex tech and engineering, explained in stories that clients and talent understand.

Three: marketing strategy that sales teams actually use.

Four: steady, credible communication when the pressure is on.

Five: a hands-on approach to AI in marketing, without losing the human voice.

(beat)

If there’s one thread through all of it, it’s translation. Between dialect and corporate English, engineers and marketers, AI tools and the people who are a little nervous about them. As editor-in-chief I learned that the best stories come from getting colleagues to tell their own. I still lead that way.

EXT. Anywhere but the office – weekend

KEVIN builds small games with AI (they live in the Lab) and is still proud of OpgewekTienen. He works in Dutch, French and English, and he’s always up for a conversation about tech, storytelling or what makes a team work.
