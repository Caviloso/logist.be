FADE IN:

EXT. Tienen – 2012

The sugar town in the Hageland. Every two weeks, Tienen Actueel prints a column by KEVIN LOGIST, with a generous helping of Tienen dialect.

Kevin

(translating)

Gine IJle. That’s Tienen dialect for “that’s quite something”.

Insert – the columns

Nineteen columns about frites, giants, old survey maps and the eternal question of what’s wrong with Tienen. (Nothing, obviously.)

They’ll appear here one by one: the Dutch original, an English translation, and a bit of context for anyone who has never been to the sugar town.
